# Original white-model acceptance checklist

- [ ] Shot ids, exact frame intervals, total duration, roles, action beats, and ending state are fixed.
- [ ] Defaults and creative assumptions are explicitly labeled.
- [ ] Scene units, floor, camera, output root, articulated roles, and persistent props are explicit.
- [ ] Full preview playback and adjacent frames at transitions/contacts were inspected.
- [ ] Gait phase, planted feet, floor penetration, contact timing, prop continuity, camera visibility, and ending stability have statuses.
- [ ] `.blend` persistence and preview dimensions/FPS/frame count/duration/hash were verified as required.
- [ ] Optional provider upload, charge, task id, and generated-result acceptance are separate from local evidence.
- [ ] Unsupported platforms/providers remain `UNVERIFIED`.
